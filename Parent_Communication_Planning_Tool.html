Part 1: Parent Resource Quick Reference

Review these resources and check the ones you plan to use with families:

📚 Parent Center Hub

Best for: Parents who want comprehensive understanding of transition

Format: Website (English and Spanish)

Key topics: IDEA definition, transition services, five domains of adult life, activities families can do at home

🎓 PACER National Parent Center

Best for: Parents of middle/high school students who want structured learning

Format: Online learning modules

Key topics: Understanding IDEA/IEPs, supporting the IEP team, college and employment planning

📄 Parent Guide to Transition Planning (PDF)

Best for: Parents who prefer printable materials they can reference

Format: Downloadable PDF

Key topics: Four domains, parent's role as advocate/mentor/life coach, common concerns

📚 Transition Resources for Parents (NASET)

Best for: Parents who want comprehensive coverage of multiple transition topics

Format: Web-based resource collection with links

Key topics: Age of Majority, employment, postsecondary education, SSI/Social Security, self-determination, testing accommodations

🎬 I'm Determined Parent Video Series

Best for: All parents; especially those who prefer video over reading

Format: Short videos (under 5 minutes each)

Key topics: Why student involvement matters, supporting self-determination, partnering with schools
