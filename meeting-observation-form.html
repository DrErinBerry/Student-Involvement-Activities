Meeting Observation Form

Student Involvement in the IEP — Oklahoma State Department of Education

This form helps the teacher or case manager observe and reflect on a student’s participation during a single IEP meeting. It focuses on the IEP participation skills from the Student Progress Rubric and captures only what the student actually did this meeting. If a skill did not come up, mark it Not observed — that simply means there was no chance to show it, not that the student fell short. Use the form across several meetings to document progress and celebrate growth.
