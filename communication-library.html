A set of ready-to-use message templates for the people you communicate with as you build student involvement in the IEP: families, administrators, colleagues, and students themselves. 4. Student Invitations

Age-appropriate invitations that help students feel welcomed and prepared for their own meetings — not surprised by them.

Inviting a student to their IEP meeting

Give to the student a week or two before the meeting

Hi ,

You are invited to an important meeting about YOU and your goals at school. It is called your IEP meeting, and it is happening on at in .

This meeting is a chance for you to share what you are good at, what helps you learn, and what you want for your future. You do not have to do it alone — we will get ready together beforehand, and you can share as much or as little as you want. Your voice matters, and we want to hear it.

I am looking forward to planning with you!

These templates are starting points — adapt the wording, tone, and reading level to fit your students, families, and setting. Created for the Oklahoma State Department of Education Student Involvement in the IEP professional learning series.
