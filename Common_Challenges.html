🔧 Problem-Solving Common Challenges

Develop creative solutions for real-world IEP implementation scenarios

📝 Activity Instructions (10 minutes)

Work through these common scenarios and brainstorm practical solutions. There's no single right answer - your creative solutions might help others facing similar challenges!

📅 Scenario 1: Scheduling Conflicts

The Challenge: You want to involve Maria, a 10th grade student, in her IEP meeting, but it's scheduled during her AP Chemistry class - the only time all required team members can attend. Missing this class would mean missing a important lab that can't be made up.

🤔 Consider These Questions:

  • What about recording or pre-meeting preparation?
  • Could the student present something ahead of time?

📋 Scenario 2: Paperwork Overwhelm

The Challenge: You're the case manager for 25 students. You want to involve students in their IEPs, but you're already working evenings and weekends just to complete the required paperwork. You're worried that adding student preparation will push you past your breaking point.

  • Could this be integrated into existing activities?

👥 Scenario 3: Parent and Student Resistance

The Challenge: You want to involve James, a 7th grader, in his IEP meeting. However, his parents say "We don't want him to feel different or know about his disability," and James himself says "I don't want to go to a boring meeting with a bunch of adults."

✨ Pro Tips for Problem-Solving:

  • Start small: You don't have to solve everything at once
  • Collaborate: Other teachers might have solutions you haven't thought of
  • Pilot first: Try solutions with one student before scaling up
  • Document what works: Build a toolkit of successful strategies
  • Be flexible: What works for one student might need tweaking for another
