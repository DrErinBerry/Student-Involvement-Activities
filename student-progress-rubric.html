Student Progress Rubric

Student Involvement in the IEP — Oklahoma State Department of Education

This rubric tracks a student’s growth in self-determination and IEP participation skills over time. The student and teacher each rate the same skills side by side, and you revisit the rubric at several points across the year so you can see progress and celebrate growth. Comparing the two columns also opens great conversations about where the student and teacher see things differently.

Each check-in column is split into two: S = the student’s own rating, T = the teacher’s rating. Fill in the date at the top of each check-in. Three check-ins are provided — for example, fall, winter, and spring.
