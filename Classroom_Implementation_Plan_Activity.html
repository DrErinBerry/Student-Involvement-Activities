Step 5: Make a SMART Goal

A SMART goal is Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound.

Example: "By December 15th, I will implement Intro to Transition Bell Ringers three times per week with my 12 transition students, and students will complete journals showing increased awareness of transition goals. I will measure success by reviewing student journal entries monthly."

Format: By [DATE], I will [SPECIFIC ACTION] with [SPECIFIC STUDENTS] resulting in [MEASURABLE OUTCOME].
